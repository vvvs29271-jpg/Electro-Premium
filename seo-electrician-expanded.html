Our Process, Guarantees, and What to Expect

Our service process begins with a safety-first evaluation. For every job we perform a visual inspection, test relevant circuits, and explain findings in plain language. For larger projects such as panel upgrades, whole-house rewires, or commercial installations, we provide a written estimate that outlines scope, timeline, and any permit work required. We schedule work to minimize disruption, protect flooring and contents, and leave the site clean at completion.

We understand that electrical projects can be stressful, especially when safety is involved. That’s why we prioritize communication: you’ll know who will be on site, what they will do, and when the work will be complete. For emergency repairs, we provide documented repair notes and photos when applicable, so you have a record for insurance or resale purposes. All work is performed to the most current NEC (National Electrical Code) and local jurisdiction requirements.

Technical services we commonly perform include load calculations to determine if a panel upgrade is needed, selective rewiring to isolate and repair problematic branch circuits, installation of AFCI/GFCI protection to reduce fire and shock risk, and specialized installations such as EV chargers that require coordination with vehicle service specifications and local permitting.

Energy efficiency is a frequent request. We can retrofit homes with energy-saving LED lighting, install smart controls that reduce wasted energy, and recommend surge protection to safeguard electronics. For commercial clients, we design lighting retrofits and controls that lower operating costs while improving illumination quality.

Our warranty and follow-up process keeps you protected. We offer workmanship warranties on installations and assist with manufacturer warranty registration for equipment like EV chargers and generators. Routine maintenance agreements are available for commercial properties, where scheduled inspections and minor repairs prevent costly downtime.

Finally, we make sure costs and options are clear. If financing or phased upgrades are advisable, we’ll present options so you can make an informed decision. We also assist customers with rebate program guidance and incentive paperwork when local utilities or state programs offer incentives for energy-efficient upgrades.
